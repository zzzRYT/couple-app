Delete in the app

  1. In Settings, choose "Delete account" in the "Account" group near the bottom.
  2. Read the consequences and turn on "I understand".
  3. Press "Sign in again with Apple to confirm". Once you are confirmed, the deletion runs. An internet connection is required.

What happens

Once the request is accepted, you are signed out on all devices and your sign-in details (auth account), profile information (name, birthday) and pending invite codes are deleted right away. The Apple connection is removed too. There is no grace period, no user restoration and no data export.

A shared space ends and both people lose access immediately. Its data, including your start date and custom anniversaries, is permanently deleted at once, with no retention period. Your partner sees a one-time notice, "Your partner deleted their account", without your name. Operational records without personal information, such as when a space ended or was deleted, may remain.

Cannot use the app?

Send an account deletion request to the public support email below. We reply to the email address you signed up with to confirm it is you, then delete the account. If you used Apple's "Hide My Email", the reply reaches you through that address.
