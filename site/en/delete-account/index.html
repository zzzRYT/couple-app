Delete in the app

  1. Choose Settings → Danger zone → Delete account.
  2. Read and acknowledge the consequences, then sign in again using the same method.
  3. Press the final delete button. An internet connection is required.

What happens

Once the request is accepted, you are signed out on all devices and your login identifier, personal profile fields and AI processing consent record are immediately deleted or anonymized. Final retention of consent records is under legal review. An active shared space ends and both people lose access immediately. There is no grace period or user restoration.

Ended space data remains internally on the server and is permanently deleted after 30 days, including home cover photos, generated decorations, prompt records, related reports and generation attempts. A generation-block fingerprint without personal information remains. A profile shell with personal fields cleared remains while referenced by a space and is deleted when those references disappear.
