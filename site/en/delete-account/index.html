Delete in the app

  1. In Settings, choose "Delete account" in the "Space" group near the bottom.
  2. Read and acknowledge the consequences, then sign in again using the same method.
  3. Press the final delete button. An internet connection is required.

What happens

Once the request is accepted, you are signed out on all devices and your login identifier (auth account), profile information, AI processing consent records and pending invite codes are deleted right away. Final retention of consent records is under legal review. If you signed in with Apple, the app's connection (tokens) is revoked. There is no grace period, no user restoration and no data export.

A shared space ends, both people lose access immediately, and for an account deletion it is permanently deleted at once, with no retention period: the room, home cover photo, generated decorations, prompt records, related reports and generation attempts. Your partner sees a one-time notice, "Your partner deleted their account", without your name. Records without personal information remain (when a space ended or was deleted, report fingerprints with only the type, reason and time, and generation-block fingerprints); their retention period is under legal review.
