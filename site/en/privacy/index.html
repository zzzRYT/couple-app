Privacy policy

Draft — pending legal review

This page describes the information the Aroom app actually handles today.

Information processed

  • Sign-in: the account identifier and email address from Sign in with Apple. The address may be an Apple "Hide My Email" address. We do not receive your name from Apple.
  • Profile: the name you type, and your birthday (month and day) and birth year if you choose to add them. Only you can see your birth year.
  • Shared space: your start date, the space time zone, custom anniversaries (title, date, repeat, memo) and the link between the two of you.
  • Invite codes: we store a hash, not the code itself. Attempts to enter a code are counted to stop guessing.
  • Access records: our service providers' servers may keep request records such as IP addresses.

We use no advertising or analytics tools and do not track you. We do not collect your location, contacts or photos, and nothing is sent to an AI service.

How it is used

Only to sign you in and keep your account, connect the two of you, show the same dates and anniversaries on both phones, and prevent abuse of invite codes.

Who sees it

Your connected partner sees your name, the birthday (month and day) you added, your start date and custom anniversaries. No other users can see them.

Stored only on your device

Anniversary reminders are scheduled on the device and are not sent to a server. Your language and appearance settings, widget data and a copy of the latest space data are stored on the device too. Space data and scheduled reminders are removed from the device when you sign out, disconnect, when your partner disconnects or deletes their account, and when you delete your account.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and realtime sync.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, and removing the connection when you delete your account.
  • Expo: app update delivery. The operating system, a project ID and a random per-install token are sent.
  • Cloudflare: hosting this website.

These providers process information only as needed to provide the services above and must protect it to the same standard as this policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnect: both people lose access to the space immediately, and its data is permanently deleted from the server after 30 days. Users cannot restore it themselves.
  • Delete account: your sign-in details and profile are deleted, and the spaces you belonged to are permanently deleted at once instead of after 30 days. The Apple connection is removed too.
  • Records that may remain: operational records without personal information, such as when a space ended or was deleted.

Your choices

You can change your name and birthday in Settings, and turn reminders off there too. To withdraw consent and erase your information, delete your account in the app. See Delete account.

Age

You must be 14 or older to use Aroom.
