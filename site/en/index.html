Frequently asked questions

What is Aroom?

Aroom lets two people connect to keep their relationship date and anniversaries together and see the day count.

How do we connect?

One person chooses Start fresh in the app and enters the relationship date to create an invite code. The other enters it on the invite screen. The code can be used once within 24 hours.

How long does an invite code work?

An invite code can be used once within 24 hours after it is created.

How do I set anniversary reminders?

Turn on Anniversary reminders in the app settings, and allow notifications for Aroom in your device settings.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, choose Delete account under Account in Settings and complete identity verification. See the Delete account page for details.
