Support

How do we connect?

One of you chooses "Start fresh" and enters your start date to get an 8-character invite code. The other chooses "I have an invite code" and enters it. A code works once, within 24 hours.

How do I change our start date?

Go to "Us" in Settings. The change applies to both of you.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen to edit it, then pick Aroom from the widget list. Open the app and finish connecting first.

Reminders are not arriving

Check that Anniversary reminders are on under "Notifications" in Settings, and that notifications for Aroom are allowed in the iPhone Settings app. Changes your partner makes reach reminders on this device the next time you open the app.

How do I disconnect or delete my account?

Both are under "Account" in Settings. See Delete account for details.
