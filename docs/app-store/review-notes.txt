Aroom is a D+Day app shared by two people who connect with an invite code.

SIGN-IN
Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method. There is no username or password, so no demo credential exists. Please sign in with any Apple Account on the review device.

SEE THE CONNECTED APP WITHOUT A SECOND PERSON
A demo partner is waiting in a demo space.
1. Open the app and tap "Continue with Apple".
2. Tap "I have an invite code".
3. Enter {{INVITE_CODE}} and tap "Check code". The code works once and expires on {{INVITE_EXPIRES}}.
4. Tap "Join", enter any name, and tap through "You're connected!".
5. On the reminder prompt, tap "Turn on" or "Not now". The app works either way.

If the code was already used, use steps 1 to 2 of "IF THE CODE FAILS" below.

WHAT TO LOOK AT
- Home: days together, next anniversary, upcoming list.
- Anniversaries tab: automatic days, birthdays, seasonal days. Tap + to add your own, then edit or delete it.
- Settings > Notifications: local reminders. No push server is used.
- Widgets: touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap the add button, and pick Aroom.
- Settings > Account > Disconnect: ends the shared space for both people.
- Settings > Account > Delete account: turn on "I understand", then "Sign in again with Apple to confirm". This deletes the account and revokes the Sign in with Apple token.

IF THE CODE FAILS
1. Choose "Start fresh", enter a date and a name to create your own space. You can see the home screen and the invite flow there.
2. Contact jinjinstar3@gmail.com and we will issue a new code.
If the app says you are already in a space, go to Settings > Account > Disconnect first.

WHY SIGN-IN IS REQUIRED
The dates and anniversaries are shared between two phones, so each person needs an account.

CONTENT
Names and anniversaries are visible only to the two connected people. There is no public feed, no chat, no discovery and no way to find or match with new people. A space is joined only with a code one person gives the other.

OTHER
The app is iPhone only and portrait only. There are no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and no AI features.
