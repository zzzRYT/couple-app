Aroom is a D+Day app for two people counting their days together.
Enter the day you got together, invite your partner, and both phones show the same D+Day and anniversaries.

D+Day
Counts from your start date as Day 1, so you always know what day you are on.

Anniversaries
Works out 50, 100, 150, 200 and 300 days and every yearly anniversary for you. Your birthdays and seasonal days such as Christmas sit in the same list.

Your own days
Add a day that is just yours with a title, date and memo. Repeat it every year if you like. Your partner sees it too.

Reminders
Get a reminder on the day and an early reminder one day, three days or a week before. You choose the time.

Widgets
See your D+Day and what is coming up on your Home Screen and Lock Screen without opening the app.

Getting started
Aroom is made for two. Sign in with Apple, then one of you creates an 8-character invite code and the other enters it to connect.

Available in Korean, English and Japanese, with dark mode.
